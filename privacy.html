    This policy applies to the website buddhistapps.vercel.app. It explains what information is collected when you use the site and what it is used for. You do not need to create an account or give personal details such as your name or email to use the site.

    1. Information we collect

    Google Analytics (only if you accept). We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. It collects anonymous statistics such as pages viewed, time spent on the site, device and browser type, and approximate geographic area. It does not run until you choose "Accept" in the cookie notice. We have enabled IP anonymisation.

    Download counts (Firebase). We use Google Firebase Realtime Database to show how many times each book has been downloaded. Only the count is stored — not information about you. However, as with any web service, your IP address and browser details are received by Google for technical reasons when your browser connects to Google's servers.

    What is stored on your device. Your browser's local storage keeps your theme choice (Dark / Light), your language choice and your cookie choice. These are not sent anywhere outside your device.

    2. How we use information

    • To improve the site and to identify the most popular books and apps.
    • To display download counts.
    • To remember your theme, language and cookie choices.

    We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

    3. Third-party services

    This site uses the services below. Their own privacy policies apply:

    • Google Analytics — anonymous usage statistics (only with your consent).
    • Google Firebase — download counts.
    • Google Fonts — to display text. Your browser requests font files from Google's servers.
    • Vercel and GitHub Pages — hosting for the site, app icons, images and PDF files.
    • Google Play, APKPure — external links for downloading apps. Their policies apply once you visit those sites.

    4. Your choices

    • You can change your Analytics decision at any time with the Cookie settings button below.
    • Clearing your browser's site data removes the choices stored on your device.
    • You can also use Google's browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics.

    5. Children

    This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

    6. About the apps (Android / Web)

    This policy applies to this website. Each app available through the site may have its own privacy notes.

    7. Changes to this policy

    This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date above will change.

    8. Contact

    If you have questions about this policy, reach us through the Contact page or by email at email.
